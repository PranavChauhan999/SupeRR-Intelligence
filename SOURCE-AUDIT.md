# Independent source audit

The organiser's Round 1 PDF requires separate ordered batting and bowling top tens on Unstop and a methodology document. It describes IPL and T20I coverage from 2022. Its ranking is a measure of performance relative to usual levels. Auction release, nationality, price, availability and squad constraints are separate scouting inputs.

## Population and joins

The six CSVs contain 200,999 deliveries, 883 match metadata rows, 141 competitions, 353 competition/team records, 6,413 competition/team/player records and 28,381 XI records. Match dates span 22 January 2022 through 1 November 2026. Only 867 matches have any deliveries. Of these, 367 IPL matches contribute 87,687 rows, 470 T20Is contribute 106,327 rows, and 30 Emerging Teams/Rising Stars matches contribute 6,985 rows. The latter use format T20 and must be explicitly identified. There are 38 super-over deliveries, 22 matches with only the first innings present, and 16 metadata-only matches.

Match IDs and competition IDs are unique, all delivery match IDs resolve, all match competition IDs resolve, and every striker joins to the competition/team/player roster. The compound delivery keys using either innings delivery sequence or over/ball are unique. The XI table has 40 extra records on match/team/player keys; use a deduplicated existence join. Three striker labels refer to two distinct IDs each (Abdul Samad, Basil Hameed and Haider Ali); Basil Hameed also identifies two bowler IDs. Across roles, order by ID after score for deterministic ties. Batting positions reach 13, so a lower-order grouping should be open-ended.

## Scorekeeping

Team runs = runs + extras1_runs + extras_2_runs. Batter runs = runs. The 264 'other' events all have one primary extra, can include bat runs, and all 224 such non-final-over deliveries fall in overs that return to six legal balls when treated as no-balls. This is a supported inference about the provider encoding. Treat 'wide', 'Overstep' and 'other' as illegal and add their primary extras to the bowler's debit. The 35 Overstep/other rows with secondary extras account for 83 runs; attribute them to the team only. Two untyped extras contribute one team run each. The only penalty row is five team runs. MCC Law 18.10.3 debits the bowler only bat runs, no-ball extras and wides (MCC, Laws of Cricket, 2017 Code). A striker's faced count convention must be explicit and consistent.

The source has three 5-ball non-final overs (match 14130, both innings over 14; 14164 innings 1 over 14) and one 7-ball non-final over (41509 innings 1 over 12). Preserve or quarantine source rows rather than fabricating deliveries. Twelve non-wicket records carry dismissed player IDs. One Retire Out (40709) has no dismissed player ID. Of 10,881 wicket events, 274 have a known non-striker as the dismissed player (271 run outs, two retired out and one obstructing event), plus the one unattributable retired out. Distinguish striker wicket hazard from all personal dismissals and state which is measured. Credit only caught/bowled/LBW/stumped/hit wicket to bowlers. Retire-outs and obstructing the field count as team wickets only for this purpose. Both 'Obstracting' and 'Obstructing' appear.

## Revisions, dates and reconciliation

The provider's two 'revised_overs' fields are multiples of six between 30 and 114, consistent with BALLS, not overs. 34 distinct matches have at least one revision field. Four additional result strings record D/L or DLS even though all three revision fields are empty (20182, 23674, 24822, 28334). Three further completed matches have a first innings of 72 or 96 legal balls and fewer than ten wickets without an explicit revision (15795, 31278, 48514). The effective delivery for a rain revision is unavailable. Avoid backfilling final targets or quotas into earlier balls; isolate complete standard matches for ranked exposure if necessary.

Across eligible competition types, both innings present, no revision indicator/result string, and excluding ties/super-over results, 384 of 385 parseable run margins and 389 of 390 parseable wicket margins agree with the deliveries. Match 14130 has an event margin of 37 vs a stated margin of 36; 20190 has 5 wickets remaining vs a 6-wicket victory in the result string. This is an independent consistency check against match result text, not the comparison of two complete scorecards (the files do not contain independently reported innings totals). Parsing needs to handle spacing, 'Wkts', and 'Won 36 Runs'. Winner directions match for completed result-bearing checked matches. Several nine-wicket innings end early, which may reflect a batter unavailable to return; avoid blanket ten-wicket completion assumptions.

## Inference

Use pre-ball legal-ball, score and wicket counters; chase target only from completed first innings for unmodified matches. Fit baseline strata from past match dates with defined fallbacks. Avoid using current-season postdating deliveries, the final winner, timestamps reflecting ball results or actual later batting orders as predictors of earlier balls. Current striker batting position at the ball can be known. Calendar-year splits avoid ambiguous season labels such as 2022/23. A chronological check should report out-of-time outcomes, coverage and strength, preferably compared with a simple baseline. Where prior-score reproducibility is weak, present the rankings as historical evidence.

Sample-size checks need both usual and pressure exposure, plus material match thresholds; report both counts and player coverage. Cluster-bootstrap whole matches, ideally common draws when comparing players or producing rank probabilities. If expectations, qualification, cutoffs or rankings are held fixed across draws, state conditionality. IPL/T20I adjustment only controls average environment; selection quality, opposition, venue, match roles and IPL transferability remain potential confounders. Display pressure IPL share, uncertainty and sensitivity, and compare within role. An interval on a score is not a probability of auction success.
